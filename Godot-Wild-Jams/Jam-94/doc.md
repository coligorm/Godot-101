# Plan

## Overview

**Theme**: Mutation

**Definition**: A mutation is a *permanent*, *random change* in an organism's DNA sequence. These alterations serve as the ultimate source of genetic diversity and drive *evolution*.

**Synonym**: Variant, Anomaly, Mutant, Modification, Transformation, Variation, Alteration

**Games that have Mutation**: 

- Prototype: You play as Alex Mercer, a shapeshifter whose body is a biological weapon, allowing you to mutate your limbs into giant blades, shields, and hammers.
- BioShock: Players inject genetic serums called Plasmids to mutate their DNA, granting them the ability to shoot lightning, fire, and swarms of insects from their hands
- The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt: Geralt uses toxic mutagen potions collected from monsters to permanently alter his genetics, unlocking powerful combat and magical abilities.
- Nuclear Throne: A fast-paced roguelike where your post-apocalyptic character gains a new, game-changing physical mutation (like extra eyes, melting skin, or heavier armor) at the end of every level.

## Ideas

### 1

- Roguelike/lite
- Top-down, dungeon crawler, clear rooms and advance
- Enemies and player mutate the more they die
- More they mutate the deathlier they get
- The more they kill, the healthier and stronger they get
- Two stats: XP and Decay
    - XP: raises Level. IF level increases, points into health, regen, stamina, Resistance
    - Decay: raises Mutantation. If Mutation level increases, give point to gain a new mutation and permanantly upgrades character, usually damage output, new skill.
- Enemies also have both these stats
- The more you die to a type of enemy, the stronger they become
- The more you kill a type of enemy, the deathlier they become

### 2

- Turn-based Action
- Top-down
- Player has the ability to mutate into different animals
- Every level-up, get option of 3 different animals to pick from
    - Turtle: health and armour, move slow on land
    - Hare: Fast, Agile, Quick Attack speed,
    - Giraffe: Greater Look disance
    - Gorrila: Strong, aggro enemies quicker
    - etc.
