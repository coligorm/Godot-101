## Ideas

Taking brainstorm and applying the topics that jumped out at me into ideas

### Charging up power

1. **Bull charging**: Spainish bull racing. Play as bull, charging down the street trying to hit people. 
2. **Spaceship power**: FTL-style power management. Swap charges to different areas of the ship to naviagte battles and space

### In-charge

3. **Office management**: deligate work to employees based on their stats/personalities to determine who is best of the job

### Cost charge

4. **Farmers' market**: Weigh fruit/veg, multiply weight by cost per kg and charge customer to correct prices wihtout.

## Verdict

Initally liking the bull-charge idea for its simplicity, but the farmers' market idea stuck out as more fun to make and expand upon.
